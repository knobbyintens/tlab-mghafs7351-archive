# ART-2024-112 - Revision v1
Recorded: 2024-08-19
Title: Neural Operators for Boundary Value Problems
Authors: Peter Nwosu, Ingrid Solberg
Venue: SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing

## Abstract
Neural operators approximate solution maps of partial differential equations, but standard training assumes boundary conditions are fixed across samples. We introduce a boundary-conditioned operator that accepts variable Dirichlet and Neumann data as additional inputs and show that it generalizes to unseen boundary configurations on Poisson and Helmholtz problems, reducing boundary error by up to an order of magnitude relative to simple feature concatenation. The approach also improves data efficiency, reaching baseline accuracy with half as many training solves.
