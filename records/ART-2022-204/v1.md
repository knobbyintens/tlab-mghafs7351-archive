# ART-2022-204 - Revision v1
Recorded: 2022-11-08
Title: Sparse Expert Routing under Distribution Shift
Authors: Nadia Petrova, Julian Mbeki, Hannah Osterloh
Venue: Journal of Machine Learning Research

## Abstract
Mixture-of-experts models route tokens to a small subset of parameters, but routing decisions learned under the training distribution become brittle when topic proportions shift at deployment. We introduce a shift-aware routing regularizer that penalizes assignments whose entropy collapses under worst-case perturbations of the input mixture, and we prove that it bounds expected routing regret by the total variation distance between training and deployment distributions. Across three controlled text-classification benchmarks with induced distribution shift, the regularized router retains 94 percent of in-distribution accuracy while improving out-of-distribution accuracy by 6.8 points over a tuned top-two baseline, with no measurable increase in inference cost.
