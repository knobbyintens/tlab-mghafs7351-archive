# ART-2023-074 - Revision v1
Recorded: 2023-07-03
Title: A Unified Theory of Catastrophic Forgetting in Continual Learning
Authors: Irena Novak, Gabriel Santos, Yuki Tanabe
Venue: Nature Machine Intelligence

## Abstract
Continual learning systems forget earlier tasks, and the many proposed remedies are rarely connected by a common theory. We show that a broad class of regularization and replay methods can be viewed as approximate gradient projections onto a task-relevant subspace, and we derive a unified bound on forgetting in terms of subspace overlap and step size. The bound explains why replay schedules matched to task similarity outperform uniform replay, a prediction we confirm on standard vision benchmarks. We also identify regimes where projection-based methods provably cannot help.
