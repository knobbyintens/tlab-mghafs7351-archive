# ART-2024-008 - Revision v1
Recorded: 2024-01-09
Title: Benchmarking Multimodal Retrieval for Scientific Figures
Authors: Jonas Wexler, Amara Diallo
Venue: arXiv preprint

## Abstract
We introduce a benchmark of 12,400 figure-caption pairs drawn from open-access physics and biology articles, annotated with retrieval intents such as locating a diagram of a specific mechanism or a plot comparing experimental methods. We evaluate contrastive and generative retrievers and find that layout-aware encoders outperform text-only models by 11 points in recall at ten, while all models struggle with multi-panel figures where a single caption describes several subfigures. The benchmark, annotation guidelines, and evaluation scripts are released to support future work on scientific document retrieval.
