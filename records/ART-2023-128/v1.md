# ART-2023-128 - Revision v1
Recorded: 2023-10-17
Title: Efficient Sampling for Reaction-Diffusion Models
Authors: Lucia Bianchi, Omar Haddad
Venue: Workshop on Generative Models for Scientific Data, NeurIPS 2024

## Abstract
Diffusion models for reaction-diffusion systems require many denoising steps because the forward process mixes slow and fast chemical modes at a single rate. We propose a mode-adaptive noise schedule that allocates more steps to slowly decaying modes and fewer to fast ones, cutting the number of function evaluations by 45 percent on three reaction-diffusion benchmarks at matched sample quality. We also describe a warm start that reuses the terminal state of a coarse run, and report where the schedule breaks down when reaction rates vary sharply in space.
